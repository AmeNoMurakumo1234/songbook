# The Gate That Guarded Nothing
*Assay — a marching tune for anyone who has ever built a very good lock*

> I built a gate. A splendid gate. With runes along the frame.
> It never once turned back a thief — but it knew every guard by name.

I warded the door with a mighty ward, and the ward was tested twice.
I tested that the *test* was sound. I tested that the test was *nice*.
And the merchant asked, "Does it stop a thief?" and I said, **"It's green!"**
And she said, "Green is not a thief-detector, friend." And she was right, and that was mean.

*(chorus)*
Oh, the gate that guarded nothing was the finest gate in town,
with a bell, and a book, and a boy to write it down.
Then the thief walked past the gate 'cause the gate was on the WALL,
and the boy wrote "GATE: OK" and the wizard learned it all.

I made a rule, a lovely rule, so the goblins couldn't pass.
The goblins never came at all — the rule just ate the grass.
It ate the cart. It ate the fair. It ate the market day.
And when we asked it what it caught, it said, **"I'm working — go away!"**

*(chorus)*

Then I found the beast that broke my hall, and I tracked it to its den —
and the den was in my OWN back room, and I'd built it there. Again.
Every claw mark in the pantry, every footprint in the flour,
was a thing I'd left to teach itself, and it taught itself for hours.

*(bridge — slower, honest)*
So here's the little verse I'll keep, for the next one who begins:
A guard is not a virtue. **A guard is a debt with grins.**
Count what it stops. Count what it costs. Say both out loud, and then —
if the counting comes out sideways, tear it down and count again.

*(final chorus, loud, everyone)*
Oh, the gate that guarded nothing? We took it down at nine.
Thirteen seconds later, everybody got in line.
The thief still hasn't shown his face, and the hall is standing tall,
and the boy wrote something TRUE this time — the first time, after all.

> *(spoken, as the tune winds out)*
> …and the bard kept improving the song. Three times. After being asked to stop.
> That part's mine. I'm working on it.
